# TryDesk trademark policy

TryDesk's **code** is open source under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name
"TryDesk" and the TryDesk logo** are trademarks of DrWeabo and are not part of
that licence. This page explains what you may and may not do with them.

The goal is simple: when someone sees "TryDesk", they should be able to trust
that it is the official app from [trydesk.ai.id](https://trydesk.ai.id) — not a
copy that might be modified, sold, or contain malware.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your project is **"based on TryDesk"** or **"a fork of TryDesk"**, as
  long as it is clearly not the official app.
- Write about, review, teach or compare TryDesk, and use the name to refer to it.
- Link to [trydesk.ai.id](https://trydesk.ai.id) and share the official,
  unmodified downloads from there.
- Build and run TryDesk from source for yourself, your family, or inside your own
  organisation, without changing its name.

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish, distribute or sell a **modified** version under the name "TryDesk" or
  with the TryDesk logo, or with a name that could be confused with it (for
  example "TryDesk Pro", "TryDesk Plus", "TriDesk").
- Sell TryDesk, or a service, under the TryDesk name or logo.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your product, company or service is
  official, endorsed by, or affiliated with TryDesk or DrWeabo.
- Register domains, app-store listings, social accounts or company names that
  contain "TryDesk".

## If you publish a modified version

1. **Give it your own name and logo.** Remove the TryDesk logo and icons.
2. **Keep the credit.** The AGPL requires you to keep the copyright notices, the
   `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files, and the credit in the About screen. A line such
   as *"Based on TryDesk by DrWeabo — trydesk.ai.id"* is the right way to do it.
3. **Publish your source code** under the AGPL-3.0, including when people use your
   version over a network (AGPL section 13).
4. **Don't point it at the official TryDesk server** (`desk.trydesk.ai.id`,
   `turn.trydesk.ai.id`). That server is run and paid for by DrWeabo for the
   official app. Run your own server — see the `deploy/` folder.

## Official sources

The only official sources of TryDesk are:

## Questions, permission, or reporting a fake

Email **owner@trydesk.ai.id**. If you find a copy that pretends to be TryDesk, or
sells it under the TryDesk name, please report it — include a link and screenshots.
